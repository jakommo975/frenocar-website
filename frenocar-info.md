# FRENOCAR

## About the Company

FRENOCAR is a Slovak family business established in **1996**, headquartered in **Novoť, Slovakia**. The company specializes in manufacturing and renovating friction materials for vehicles and industrial equipment.

## Core Services & Products

### Brake Materials
Production of a wide range of brake shoes, pads, and linings for cars, trucks, and agricultural machinery. They also offer custom solutions for non-standard components.

### Clutch Components
Manufacturing of lamella clutches for vehicles and utility machines, specializing in ceramic-metallic variants with superior friction properties at elevated temperatures.

### Metal Fabrication
- Conventional and CNC machining (steel and cast iron)
- Plasma and oxy-acetylene cutting
- Tool manufacturing and sharpening

### Spare Parts for Agricultural, Garden & Forestry Machinery
One of the company's major focuses is the sale of spare parts for agricultural machinery (Zetor), garden machinery (Tera, Agzat), and forestry machinery (LKT).

### Powder Metallurgy
Production of sintered metal components — offering waste reduction and faster production of complex geometries.

### Freno Flux Engine
The company designs and manufactures the **Freno Flux Engine** — an axial flux electric motor. Axial flux technology is known for its exceptional efficiency and compact size compared to conventional radial flux motors. While the underlying technology is not new, Frenocar has engineered and refined its own production to deliver a high-quality, well-built motor.
